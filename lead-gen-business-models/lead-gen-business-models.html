Lead Generation
Overview

Broker

Middleman between lead sellers and buyers. Buy low, sell high, pocket the spread.

Partnership

Control one side (buyers OR lead gen), partner handles the other. Split the profit.

Full Stack

Own everything — ads, funnels, tracking, qualification. Maximum control and margin.

Model 1

The Broker Model

Middleman between lead sellers and buyers

  • Buy leads at $150, sell at $250, pocket $100
  • Profit margin: 30-50%
  • Time investment: 5-10 hours/week
  • Skills needed: Communication, networking, negotiation
  • No ad spend, no tech stack, no lead generation

Model 2

The Partnership Model

You control one side, partner handles the other. Split the profit.

  • Path A: You bring the buyers, partner generates the leads
  • Path B: You generate the leads, partner brings the buyers
  • Shared risk, shared reward
  • Great for building skills while earning
  • Learn the full pipeline without owning it all

Which One Should You Pick?

  • No ad experience → Start as a Broker
  • Some skills, not all → Partnership Model
  • Media buying + sales → Go Full Stack
Most people start as brokers, learn the model, then shift to full stack.
