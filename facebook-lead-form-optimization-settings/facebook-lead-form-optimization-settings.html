FACEBOOK ADS BREAKDOWN

The Facebook Lead Form Setting
Nobody Uses (But Should)

Maximize Number of Leads vs Maximize Number of Qualified Leads

THE SETUP

Every lead form campaign tells the algorithm what to chase

  • Two settings: Maximize Number of Leads vs Maximize Number of Qualified Leads
  • Same form, same ad, same audience, different instructions
  • Most accounts default to volume and nobody ever checks
OPTION ONE

Chases raw volume at the lowest cost per lead

  • Doesn't care who fills out the form or why
  • Doesn't distinguish curious clicks from real prospects
  • Works fine for low-ticket offers where any lead has value
THE CATCH

A great cost-per-lead number can hide a terrible close rate

  • Every lead still needs a call, follow up, a real conversation
  • Cheap leads still cost your sales team time
  • The savings just move from the ad account to the sales calendar
OPTION TWO

Optimizes toward people who resemble your real customers

  • Learns from screening questions in the instant form
  • Or a feedback loop from your CRM on which leads became real deals
  • Like training a new hire on what a good customer looks like
UNDER THE HOOD

Two signal sources teach the algorithm what a real customer looks like

  • Instant form: real qualifying questions let people self-disqualify
  • CRM side: sales marks a lead real opportunity or dead end
  • Over time the algorithm learns the pattern behind who converts
THE MISTAKE

Turning on qualified optimization with no feedback loop feeding it

  • No screening questions and no CRM connection means nothing to learn from
  • The algorithm ends up optimizing blind, on guesses
  • Can perform worse than just leaving it on the basic volume setting
THE FIX

Match the setting to the business, in the right order

  • Low-ticket, high-volume offer → Maximize Number of Leads is fine
  • Real sales process behind the form → Maximize Number of Qualified Leads
  • Build the screening questions or CRM feedback loop FIRST, then switch

What To Remember

  1. Every lead form campaign runs on an optimization goal, volume or quality
  2. Maximize Number of Leads chases volume, fine for low-ticket offers
  3. Cheap leads still cost your sales team real time
  4. Maximize Number of Qualified Leads learns from screening questions or CRM feedback
  5. Feed it real data first, then flip the switch, never the other way around
