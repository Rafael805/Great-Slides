The System

Four questions decide if a car accident lead is worth a dollar

  • When? Hurt? Fault? Lawyer?
  • 3 of them can rescue a good case
  • The rest is just contact info
Question 1 · Flag & Ship

Every state has a deadline to file. Miss it, the case is dead.

  • Over 1 year old = likely past the line
  • We don't silently kill it
  • We flag it so the buyer decides
Question 2 · Saver

"I wasn't injured" normally ends it. Most people don't know what counts.

  • No injury normally kills the case
  • But whiplash and soft-tissue count
  • A symptom checklist rescues real cases
Question 3 · Saver

"It was my fault" is an instant reject for most funnels. Not for us.

  • Other driver speeding or texting? Case lives
  • Partly at fault can still recover
  • Hit-and-run is the hard reject — no one to sue
Question 4 · Saver

"I already have a lawyer" normally ends it. We ask one more thing.

  • Is that lawyer for THIS accident?
  • Often it's a different matter — rescued
  • Already settled or dropped = hard reject

The Scoreboard

  1. Q1 — When did it happen? Flag & ship
  2. Q2 — Were you hurt? RESCUE
  3. Q3 — Was it your fault? RESCUE (hit-and-run rejects)
  4. Q4 — Do you have a lawyer? RESCUE (settled/dropped rejects)

4 questions. 3 with a built-in rescue. Filter hard, rescue smart.
