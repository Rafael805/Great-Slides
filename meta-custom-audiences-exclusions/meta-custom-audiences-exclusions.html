The Basics

A custom audience is just a list of your own people

  • Built from customers, leads, visitors
  • Drops into include or exclude
  • The exclude slot is the empty box
Benefit 1

Stop paying to reach people who already converted

  • Past leads keep seeing your ad
  • Exclude them, save the spend
  • Every dollar hits a new prospect
Benefit 2

Keep your ad fresh and your costs down

  • Repeats burn out your audience
  • High frequency tells Meta you're boring
  • Exclusions keep delivery cheap
Benefit 3

The ghost wrecking your lookalikes is your own customers

  • Lookalikes copy your seed list
  • Dirty seed clones the wrong crowd
  • Clean seed, exclude current customers
Benefit 4

Get a cost per lead you can finally trust

  • Returning buyers fake-cheapen your CPL
  • Exclusions reveal true new-lead cost
  • Trustworthy data, confident decisions

Four Wins So Far

  2. Less ad fatigue, lower delivery cost
  3. Smarter lookalikes that find real prospects
