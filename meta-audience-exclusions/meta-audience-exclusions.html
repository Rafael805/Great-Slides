Your Campaigns Are Competing Against Each Other

How Meta audience exclusions work — and why skipping them is burning your CPL

The Basics

Tell Meta exactly who not to show your ads to

  • Set at the ad set level inside Meta Ads Manager
  • Built from custom audiences you already have
  • Runs automatically — no ongoing management required

Both Campaigns Bid Against Each Other

Meta runs an auction — your two campaigns compete, driving up your own CPL

  • One budget funds two campaigns fighting for the same person
  • Your CPL climbs without any change in creative or targeting
  • Meta's algorithm does exactly what you told it — you didn't tell it to skip anyone
The Fix

3 Exclusions Every Account Needs

✅

Recent Converters

Anyone who already took the action you're optimizing for — a lead, a purchase, a sign-up. Don't pay to acquire someone twice.

Protect CPL
🎯

Active Retargeting Audiences

Exclude your retargeting audiences from prospecting campaigns. Stop both campaigns from bidding on the same warm traffic.

Stop Overlap
🚫

Disqualified Traffic

Audiences that signal wrong fit for your niche — wrong geography, wrong language, low-quality engagement signals. Cut waste at the source.

Improve Quality
Step by Step

Where to find exclusions in Meta Ads Manager

  • Step 1: Create your custom audience first (converters, retargeting list, disqualified)
  • Step 2: Open your ad set — go to the Audience section and scroll to Exclusions
  • Step 3: Click Add Exclusion and select the custom audience from the dropdown

Avoid These

Exclusion errors that kill performance quietly

  • Excluding too broadly — your audience shrinks to zero reach
  • Setting exclusions at campaign level instead of ad set level — wrong scope
  • Stale audience lists that never refresh — refresh at minimum every 30 days
Your 5-Point Exclusion Audit

Open an Active Ad Set Right Now

  1. Are recent converters excluded from all acquisition campaigns?
  2. Are your retargeting audiences excluded from prospecting campaigns?
  3. Are exclusions set at the ad set level — not the campaign level?
  4. Have your audience lists been refreshed within the last 30 days?
  5. Do any two active campaigns target the same audience with no exclusions between them?
