The Problem

The market you pick matters more than your skills, funnel, or offer.

The Framework

6 Criteria for a Good Niche

# Criteria Why It Matters
1 Large segment of the population needs it Big addressable market = more leads to sell
2 High volume of leads/purchases daily Consistent demand, not seasonal spikes
3 Existing companies that need leads Buyers already exist and are spending money
4 Not limited by geography Scale beyond your local market
5 Compliant on Google and Meta ads You can actually run paid traffic
6 Sale price / LTV above $500+ High value = you can charge more per lead
Market Size

Stop chasing restaurants and gyms. Go where the money is: nationwide markets with massive volume.

Strategy

Pick a demographic, not just a niche. That's how you create a blue ocean inside a red one.

★ Best Tier

The gold standard. Highest LTV, massive demand, recurring need. These are the niches everyone should aspire to.

Legal - Personal Injury Wealth Management
A-Level Tier

Excellent niches. High LTV, strong demand, and plenty of buyers. Very profitable with the right approach.

Gold IRA IVF / Fertility Clinics Tax Relief Life Insurance Mortgage / Home Loans Short Term Opps (ERC, PPP) Senior Care Industrial Equipment
B-Level Tier

Solid niches. Good money to be made, but may have lower LTV or more competition. Where most agencies start.

Health Insurance Legal (Other Areas) Business Insurance Credit Repair Business Loans Estate Planning Personal Loans Debt Consolidation Car & Home Insurance SaaS Car Loans
C-Level Tier

Risky niches. Can work, but have major drawbacks: low LTV, geographic limits, compliance issues, or saturation.

Medicare Roofing Marketing Agencies Home Security Solar (Residential) HVAC Dental Clinics Jet / Yacht Charters Kitchen Renovation Window Installation Cybersecurity Car Warranty
⚠ Avoid

Stay away. Low LTV, tiny markets, unreliable buyers, or too much competition for too little reward.

Gyms Plumbing Coaches & Consultants Real Estate Restaurants Chiropractors Electricians
