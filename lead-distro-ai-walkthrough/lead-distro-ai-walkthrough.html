Routing Types

Three Ways to Route Your Leads

Round Robin

Leads rotate evenly across all active buyers. Simple, fair distribution with no manual management.

Priority-Based

Your top buyer gets leads first. When they hit capacity, the next buyer in the queue picks up automatically.

Cap-Based

Each buyer has a daily or monthly cap. The system respects every limit and stops routing when they're full.
