Problem 1

Works with 1 supplier and 1 buyer. Breaks the moment you scale.

  • Multiple sheets, multiple Zaps, multiple text threads
  • Copy-pasting lead data between spreadsheets
  • No idea if you're making money or losing it
Problem 2

What is your profit per lead, per supplier, in real time?

  • Duplicates, returns, and blocked leads change your real margin
  • You don't find out until end of month
  • Software shows revenue, cost, and profit in real time

Problem 3

Google Sheets and Zapier have zero duplicate detection.

  • Matches by phone, email, or custom fields
  • Configurable time windows (30 days, campaign-wide)
  • Duplicates get caught before reaching your buyer
Problem 4

Are Your Suppliers Sending Complete Data?

Mark fields as required. Incomplete leads get blocked automatically.

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • At Fault Status
  • Has Attorney
  • Injury Type
  • Trusted Form Certificate
  • Missing a field? Lead is blocked, supplier is notified
  • Your buyers always get complete, usable leads
Problem 5

The Buyer Experience

Which broker would your buyers rather work with?

Without Software

  • Shared Google Sheet via email
  • Manual status updates
  • "How many leads left?" texts
  • Return requests via email
  • Looks like a side hustle

With Buyer Portal

  • Branded login with your logo
  • Update lead statuses in dashboard
  • See balance and caps in real time
  • Submit returns with one click
  • Looks like a real operation
Trust Builder

Every lead comes with a Trusted Form certificate.

  • Proves the lead filled out a form and gave consent
  • Make it a required field from all suppliers
  • Instantly separates you from brokers who can't prove compliance
The Scaling Problem

Why most brokers stay stuck at 3-4 buyers

  • Without software: every new buyer = more sheets, more Zaps, more math
  • With software: set cap, set payout, set filters, leads flow
  • Adding buyer #10 is the same effort as buyer #1
