Do Not Be Afraid To Filter

We ask six to eight questions on every form

  • MVA example: were you injured, yes or no
  • No injury means no case, so filter them out
  • Junk volume kills a buyer faster than low volume
  • Low volume means small, junk means unreliable

Why We Built Our Own Calendar

A normal calendar books anybody who clicks. That is the problem.

  • Qualifies before a time slot is ever offered
  • The answers route the call to the right host
  • Book for a client who has no login, they connect their own calendar
  • Two way Google Calendar with conflict checking
