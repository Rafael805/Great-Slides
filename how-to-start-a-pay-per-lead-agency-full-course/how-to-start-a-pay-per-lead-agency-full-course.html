The Model

Two Ways To Sell What You Generate

  • A lead is a form fill: name, phone, email, what they need
  • A call must hit 90+ seconds to count as billable
  • Calls pay more because the bar is higher
The Model

A Simple Way To Think About It

$2,400

Building Your Brand

Buyers Pay People They Trust

  • Not just running ads from a random Facebook page
  • A real brand turns a cold pitch into a signed buyer
  • Skip it and every buyer conversation starts from doubt
Building Your Brand

What A Real Brand Actually Needs

  • A name and domain that fit the niche
  • A simple logo, nothing fancy
  • A Facebook Business structure that won't get flagged
Picking Your Niche

Test Any Niche Before You Touch It

  • Are there enough buyers who actually want these leads
  • Can you sell each lead 2.5 to 3x your cost to generate it
  • Can you get ads approved without getting banned

Offer + Pricing

Per Lead, Per Call, Per Appointment, Rev Share

Per Lead

Simplest place to start.

Per Call

Pays more, more risk if the calls are bad.

Per Booked Appointment

Highest pay, highest risk if the buyer's prospect doesn't show.

Rev Share

Paid on the close, once you have real buyer trust.

Offer + Pricing

Exclusive Is The Easier Sell

  • Exclusive: one buyer gets that lead, nobody else
  • Shared: sold to multiple buyers, harder to close
  • Price at 2.5 to 3x your cost to generate the lead
Quiz Funnel

A few short questions before the lead is captured

  • Qualifies the lead as it goes
  • Builds real intent, not a guess
  • Ends with contact info, not just a click
Quiz Funnel

Consent, Then A Thank You Page

  • Captures contact info plus consent to be contacted
  • Consent is what keeps you compliant
  • Thank you page sets expectations for what's next
Call Funnel

One giant number, one tap, one call

  • Ad has a call button, not a form
  • Landing page is one giant number to tap
  • Routes through quick qualifying before the buyer

Tracking

Track What Actually Sells, Not Just What Fills Out A Form

  • Pixel and conversions tracking must reflect real sales
  • Buyers who share which leads closed unlock quality optimization
  • This is how you outscale everyone else in the niche
Buyers + Compliance

Ads Are The Best Way To Get Buyers

  • Same mechanic as lead gen itself, run ads to business owners
  • Cold outreach, referrals, and broker networks fill in the rest
  • New buyers prepay a pilot before moving to invoicing
Buyers + Compliance

Compliance Isn't Optional

  • Documented consent from every lead
  • Disclose if calls are recorded
  • Late payment pauses delivery, no exceptions
Buyers + Compliance

Check Their Follow Up Before You Touch Your Ads

  • Check their speed to follow up first
  • Check how qualified the leads actually were
  • Most of the time the leads were fine
Scaling

Two Moves When A Buyer Wants More

  • Send more volume at the same price
  • Raise your price 10 to 20 percent
  • If they won't take more, go find that playbook in a new niche
