Pay Per Lead Playbook

Funnel Strategy + Platforms

The 2 funnel types, 4 platforms, and account rules, step by step

The Hook

Ad Spend Doesn't Die On The Ad

  • It dies in the funnel behind it
  • A great ad into a broken funnel still fails
  • Fix the funnel first, then scale the ad
The Problem

Running Ads With No Funnel Plan Burns Budget Fast

  • No qualification, so intent is a guess
  • No consent capture, a compliance risk later
  • No set expectations, buyers get surprised leads
The Promise

What You'll Walk Away With

  • 2 funnel types that actually qualify leads
  • 4 platforms to run traffic on
  • The account and budget rules that let you scale

Step 1

A few short questions before the lead is captured

  • Qualifies the lead as it goes
  • Builds real intent, not a guess
  • Ends with contact info, not just a click
Step 1 Key Insight

Qualifies The Lead, Builds Intent

  • A few short questions about their situation
  • Spending two minutes answering signals seriousness
  • Far more serious than a bare contact box
Step 1 Compliance

Consent, Then A Thank You Page

  • Captures contact info plus consent to be contacted
  • Consent is what keeps you compliant
  • Thank you page sets expectations for what's next
Step 2

One giant number, one tap, one call

  • Ad has a call button, not a form
  • Landing page is one giant number to tap
  • Routes through quick qualifying before the buyer
Step 2 Key Insight

The Caller Is Already Further Along

  • Picking up the phone takes more effort than a click
  • Quick qualifying routing before connecting to the buyer
  • Higher intent means a higher conversion rate
Step 1 vs Step 2

Quiz Funnel vs Call Funnel

DimensionQuiz FunnelCall Funnel
FormatMulti-step formTap-to-call button
Intent levelModerate to highVery high
Qualification stepQuiz questionsQuick call routing
Best forNiches with more buyers, lower price per unitNiches where calls pay a premium

Where To Run These Ads

Four platforms, four very different starting points

Step 3

The 4 Platforms

Meta

Easiest place to start. Target broad and let the algorithm do the work.

Google

Powerful but brutally competitive. You'll need a local angle to compete.

TikTok

Works well for younger audiences in the right niche.

SEO / Rank and Rent

Build a site that ranks and drives free organic traffic.

Step 4

One campaign. Broad targeting. A handful of creatives.

  • One campaign, not five
  • Broad targeting, let the algorithm work
  • Three to five creatives running at once
Step 5

$50 to $100 A Day, 72-Hour Windows

  • Start around $50 to $100 a day
  • Judge results over 72 hours, not two days
  • Early data lies, give it time
Step 6

Scale Slow, Kill Fast

20-30%
Scale winners per day
Never double your budget overnight
1.5-2x
Kill threshold
Kill a creative once it's running 1.5 to 2x your target cost with nothing to show for it

Funnels + Platforms, Locked In

  1. Step 1, Quiz funnels qualify and build intent
  2. Step 2, Call funnels convert higher, faster
  3. Step 3, Meta, Google, TikTok, and SEO are your four starting platforms
  4. Step 4, keep the account boring: one campaign, broad targeting, a few creatives
  5. Step 5 and 6, scale winners 20 to 30% a day, kill losers at 1.5 to 2x target
