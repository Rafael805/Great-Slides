Understanding The Problem

Same person fills out multiple forms, multiple vendors send them to the same buyer

  • Big law firms buy from 3-10+ vendors
  • Same accident victim found by multiple ads
  • Buyer flags it as duplicate, you eat the cost
The Scale Problem

At low volume, every returned lead is a gut punch

  • 10 leads per week: every return is devastating
  • No leverage to negotiate return caps
  • Can't absorb 30-40% return rates
Hidden Cost
"
When your buyer sees repeated duplicates, they stop trusting your lead quality, even though duplicates have nothing to do with quality.
The perception shift that kills new agencies
Fix #1

Know your duplicate risk before spending a dollar on ads

  • 2 vendors = 10-15% duplicate rate
  • 5 vendors = 25-30% duplicate rate
  • 8+ vendors = 40%+ duplicate rate
Fix #2

Never give a buyer unlimited time to return leads

  • 24-48 hour return window, max
  • Define valid reasons: duplicate, bad number
  • "Didn't like it" is NOT a valid return
Fix #3

Cross-reference every lead before sending it

  • Log every phone + email in a spreadsheet
  • Check new leads against past deliveries
  • Works at 10-20/day, breaks at 50+
The Solution

Lead Distro AI Duplicate Detection

  • Match by phone, email, or both
  • Scope per campaign or per buyer
  • Time window: 1 day, 7 days, or lifetime

The System

Four steps to a profitable PPL agency

  • Generate quality leads (targeting + creative)
  • Deliver instantly (real-time routing)
  • Block duplicates (automated dedup)
  • Track everything (analytics + reporting)

Key Takeaways

  1. Duplicates are a logistics problem, not a quality problem
  2. Ask your buyer how many vendors they use before you start
  3. Set a return window with clear rules
  4. Track leads manually at low volume (phone + email)
  5. Automate dedup with Lead Distro AI at scale
  6. Catch duplicates before they reach the buyer, not after
