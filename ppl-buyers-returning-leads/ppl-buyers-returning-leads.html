Stage 1

Every lead starts here, but this is not where margin is made

  • Form fills, click-to-calls, landing pages
  • Meta ads push traffic to the capture layer
  • Volume without signal = noise
Stage 2

Kill junk leads before they ever hit your buyer

  • Intent, geography, fraud, qualification
  • Automated, runs on every lead, every time
  • But software can't predict a signed case
Recap (30%)
"
Capture and auto-score are table stakes. Stage 3 is where the real PPL margin gets made.
The unlock is the human in the loop
Stage 3 — The Unlock

Your buyers mark every lead: accepted, contacted, qualified, retained, returned

  • Human disposition is the gold signal
  • Retained cases = ground truth
  • Most PPL agencies skip this entirely
Stage 4

Real buyer dispositions fire back to Meta with the original click ID

  • Accepted, contacted, qualified, retained
  • 10x more valuable than standard lead events
  • Meta learns what an accepted lead looks like
Stage 5

AI reads the outcome data and writes the optimization plan

  • Sees which ad sets produce retained cases
  • Spots winning zip codes, hooks, times of day
  • Writes pause and scale recommendations
Recap (70%)
"
Lead, score, buyer updates, CAPI fires, AI reads the pattern. One step left, and this is where PPL margin starts compounding.
Stage 6 is where the leverage lives
Stage 6

Optimize on accepted leads and retained cases, not cost per lead

  • Pause ad sets with high returns
  • Scale ad sets with high retention
  • Loop closes, margin compounds weekly
